Culinary Legacy of the Arabian Peninsula

Ancient Mandi Traditions & Midnight Skylounge Craft

At Arabian Corner Restaurant - Skylounge, we honor the ancient method of Mandi cooking. Meats and marinated chicken are suspended in wood-fired pits, allowing the juices to naturally baste the aromatic basmati rice steaming below, seasoned with cardamom, loomi (black lime), saffron, and royal cloves.
