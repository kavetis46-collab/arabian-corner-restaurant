Culinary Legacy of the Arabian Peninsula

Where Ancient Mandi Traditions Meet Master Charcoal Craft

At Arabian Corner Restaurant, we honor the centuries-old method of Mandi cooking. Tender lamb cuts and premium chicken are suspended inside deep, wood-fired underground pits. As the meat roasts, its flavorful drippings infuse the golden basmati rice bubbling below, infused with cardamom, dried lime (loomi), cloves, and saffron.
